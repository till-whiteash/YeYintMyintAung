StoreFlow

MAKINO · 1ST PLACE

A self-running, lights-out smart tool-room. Staff collect and return tools with an RFID card scan, replacing manual issuing and inaccurate stock counts. An edge-AI pipeline on a Raspberry Pi runs YOLO object detection with a camera and load cell, reconciling three signals — weight, image and dispense log — to auto-flag theft, jams and miscounts. A live dashboard handles stock forecasting, most-used-item analytics and attendance, with conveyor-fed auto-restocking. Built with the LocalEdge SG team; I pitched it solo at the finals.

Shifa — Health Companion

AWS SUMMIT DUBAI 2026

An agentic AI care navigator built at the AWS Agentic AI Hackathon (Future Vision) at AWS Summit Dubai 2026. Shifa turns one message or call into a triaged, pre-filled, booked hospital visit — in Arabic or English — and never diagnoses. It reads an uploaded Emirates ID to pre-fill government and insurer forms, triages against a knowledge base (red-flag symptoms route straight to emergency services), checks insurance eligibility, and a voice agent phones the clinic and insurer to confirm the booking. Built on Claude Sonnet with AWS Lambda, AgentCore Gateway, DynamoDB and Twilio, with guardrails that block diagnosis and dosing before the model runs. A four-person team; I built it as the Singapore Polytechnic member.
